Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This portfolio (tapariapurv.github.io) is a personal, non-commercial website belonging to Purv Taparia. This policy explains what little data is collected when you visit.

What is collected

This site uses Umami, a privacy-focused, cookieless analytics tool, to understand aggregate visitor counts and popular pages. Umami does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites. No personal data, IP address, or device fingerprint is stored against an identifiable visitor profile.

What is not collected

  • No account creation, login, or password data
  • No payment or financial information
  • No location tracking beyond coarse, anonymized analytics

Contact form / email

If you email tapariapurv+portfolio@gmail.com using the links on this site, that email is sent directly to a personal inbox and is used only to respond to you. It is not shared with third parties.

Third-party links

This site links out to GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, Beehiiv, Amazon, and Bribooks. Each of those services has its own privacy policy that governs any data you share with them directly.

Children's privacy

This site is the personal portfolio of a minor and is intentionally limited to publicly shareable achievements, projects, and creative work — no sensitive personal information (address, phone number, or exact date of birth) is published here.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision.

Questions
